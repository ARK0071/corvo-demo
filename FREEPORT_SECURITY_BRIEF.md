**Prepared by:** Corvo Systems for Pole Star Global  
**Project Sponsor:** Port Freeport, Brazoria County, Texas

## Project Description
 
Port Freeport is one of the fastest-growing US Gulf ports and a critical node for liquid bulk, project cargo, and containers. Its FY25 budget directs roughly $2.3M annually to security service fees, reflecting expanded manpower and contracted rates. This brief frames a federally and state-funded risk mitigation upgrade combining vessel intelligence, sanctions screening, cyber resilience for control systems and IT, and physical infrastructure hardening.
 
---
 
## Cross-Cutting Context (Digital Language, PIDP Timing)
 
- **Digital / IT / data language in grant requests: PARTIAL.** TxDOT MIP allows cyber, IT, and digital scope as eligible costs, but Freeport's own published submissions lead with physical infrastructure (Velasco Container Terminal, channel, storage). Cybersecurity language present but not prominent.
- **Harbor pilot / optimization language: GAP.** No harbor pilot optimization language found in published Channel Improvement Project documentation, board minutes, or recent PIDP narrative. Clear opportunity to introduce Pole Star scope.
- **PIDP FY25: AWARDED.** Port Freeport confirmed on the AAPA-recognized list of 18 member ports awarded in MARAD's March 2026 announcement (37 projects, $774M total).
- **PIDP FY26: OPEN.** Application deadline Monday, June 1, 2026 at 11:59:59 PM EDT. Total pool $488,628,000 (NOFO MA-PID-26-001). Previous recipients may apply for additional project phases, which gives Freeport a natural lane for next-phase digital scope. NDAA Section 3511 requires cybersecurity plan for digital infrastructure or software acquisitions, which Pole Star plus Corvo can directly support.

## Pole Star Global Plug-In
 
Pole Star Global delivers the maritime risk overlay that hardens Port Freeport's perimeter and screening posture across both grant pathways:
 
- **PurpleTRAC:** Screens vessel calls and counterparties against 1,800+ sanctions watchlists across 90+ jurisdictions, directly responsive to OFAC, BIS, and FinCEN expectations on US ports handling LNG and refined products.
- **MTI 0–5 vessel risk verdict:** Usable as real-time gate criterion.
- **Pole Star Defense ISR:** Adds coastal surveillance and dark-vessel detection.
- **BRIC fit:** Physical resilience grants offer modest direct Pole Star scope, but BRIC subapplications increasingly include monitoring and sensor layers that benefit from vessel intelligence integration.

## Corvo Plug-In (Post-Award Compliance)
 
BRIC and PSGP awards each trigger material federal compliance obligations:
 
- **2 CFR 200** uniform guidance for both, including indirect costs and procurement standards for sub-recipients
- **BABA** for storm wall steel, fencing, sensors, cameras, IT hardware
- **Davis-Bacon** prevailing wages for any covered construction
- **NEPA** environmental review for waterside or shoreline modifications
- **FEMA-specific** environmental and historic preservation reviews for BRIC
- **Single Audit and SEFA** at the federal expenditure threshold
- **DHS cybersecurity reporting** tied to PSGP
